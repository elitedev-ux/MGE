## Phone prompt

Use case: compositing. Asset: transparent landscape ecommerce hero product render. Image 1 is the edit target: preserve these two graphite phones, their camera layout and proportions. Image 2 is ONLY a reference for the wide low round brushed silver podium, camera viewpoint, premium studio lighting and composition. Produce a LANDSCAPE 1536x1024 composition with both full phones standing on a broad low silver circular display stand, all device edges and the entire stand visible with 7 percent clear margin. Arrange front and back phones slightly angled beside each other, retaining recognisable design. Phones should occupy about 70 percent of canvas height; the silver podium about 75 percent of canvas width, not a tall plinth. Neutral silver with a subtle cool blue rim highlight, blue-black screen rather than purple. Genuine transparent background outside objects and soft contact shadow; NO black background, no room, no text, no boxes, no border. Match the product-on-podium presentation in the glasses reference; do NOT include glasses. Do not crop the products or stand. This is a marketing illustration.
